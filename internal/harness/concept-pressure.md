---
name: concept-pressure
description: Keep the concept surface small and prevent agent-visible distinctions from being mistaken for deterministic behavior.
---

Proposals that add or change durable concepts must:

- name the preserved invariant,
- account for permanent propagation cost,
- identify the deterministic consumer for every proposed value, and
- choose the lowest durable Abstraction Level that preserves the invariant.

Reason: keep the concept surface small and prevent agent-visible distinctions from being mistaken for deterministic behavior.
